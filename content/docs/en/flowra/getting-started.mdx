Follow these steps to open Flowra and begin working with account opening workflows.

## Understand your role

Flowra queues are organized by role:

| Queue | Who uses it | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| Sales Queue | Sales staff | Accounts returned to Sales/Branch that need follow-up |
| CSO Queue | Compliance staff | Accounts waiting for compliance review and verification |

Your access to queues and actions depends on your assigned role and permissions. If you need access to a queue you cannot see, contact your administrator.

## First-time tasks

If this is your first time using Flowra:

1. Review the [Dashboard](./dashboard) to understand the overview metrics
2. Open the [Opening Accounts](./opening-accounts) section to learn how to create and manage account applications
3. Familiarize yourself with the document upload process in [Digital Signing & Documents](./digital-signing)
4. Set up your profile and preferences in [Account Settings](../guides/account-settings)
5. Check your notifications regularly via [Notifications](../guides/notifications)

<Callout type="info">
  The Dashboard shows a summary of your workload. For detailed account
  information and actions, open the relevant queue or account record.
</Callout>
