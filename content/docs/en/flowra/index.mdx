# Flowra

Flowra is the financial workflow automation application within CSSI. It guides sales, compliance, and operations staff through account opening, document management, and related onboarding workflows.

## What Flowra is for

- **Account opening** — Create, track, and manage new client account applications from initial submission through approval
- **Queue management** — Work through Sales and CSO queues to process pending accounts
- **Document handling** — Upload and manage required client documents (KTP, NPWP, SIUP) and supporting files
- **Workflow automation** — Move accounts through defined stages with clear handoffs between roles

## What you can do

- Create new opening account applications with detailed client information
- View and manage the Sales Queue and CSO Queue
- Track account status through the full onboarding lifecycle
- Upload required and supporting documents for each account
- Review and process accounts assigned to your role
- Monitor your workload from the Dashboard overview
- Receive notifications about account status changes

## How Flowra fits into CSSI

Flowra handles the account opening and onboarding workflow. Other applications handle different parts of the business:

- **Portal** — Central dashboard and application launcher
- **CRM** — Sales and trading operations
- **Intranet** — Internal communications
- **ITQM** — IT development requests
- **Daily Operation** — Operational checklists

## Shared features

Flowra shares some features with all CSSI applications:
