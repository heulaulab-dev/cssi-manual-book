# Agreements

Agreements are a key part of the account opening workflow in Flowra. They represent the formal documents that clients and the company sign to establish the account relationship.

## What Agreements are for

- **Formalize the account relationship** — Agreements document the terms and conditions between the client and the company
- **Track signing status** — Monitor which agreements have been signed and which are pending
- **Integrate with Digital Card Signing** — Agreements can be processed through the DCS system for electronic signatures

## How Agreements fit into the workflow

Agreements are typically generated as part of the account opening process. Once an account application is created and progresses through the workflow, the relevant agreements are prepared for review and signing.

The agreement workflow generally follows these stages:

1. **Create** — Agreement is generated based on the account details
2. **Review** — Staff reviews the agreement for accuracy and completeness
3. **Approve** — The agreement is approved for signing
4. **Sign** — The client signs the agreement (physically or via DCS)
5. **Complete** — The signed agreement is filed and the account is activated

## Agreement statuses

| Status | Description |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Agreement is being prepared or edited |
| **Pending Review** | Agreement is awaiting review by authorized staff |
| **Pending Approval** | Agreement has been reviewed and is awaiting final approval |
| **Approved** | Agreement is approved and ready for signing |
| **Signed** | Agreement has been signed by the client |
| **Void** | Agreement has been cancelled or invalidated |

## Creating an agreement

Agreements are typically created automatically when an account application progresses to the appropriate stage. In some cases, you may need to manually initiate or regenerate an agreement.

To create or view an agreement:

1. Open the relevant account from the Opening Accounts section
2. Navigate to the agreement or documents section
3. Follow the prompts to generate or review the agreement

## Reviewing an agreement

Before an agreement can be approved, it must be reviewed for accuracy:

1. Open the agreement from the account detail
2. Review all fields and terms for correctness
3. If changes are needed, return the agreement for revision
4. If the agreement is correct, proceed to approval

## Approving an agreement

Once the review is complete, the agreement can be approved for signing:

1. Confirm that all details are accurate
2. Click **Approve** to move the agreement to the approved state
3. The agreement is now ready for the client to sign

## Rejecting or voiding an agreement

If an agreement contains errors or is no longer valid:

- **Reject** — Return the agreement for revision with comments explaining what needs to be corrected
- **Void** — Permanently cancel the agreement. This action cannot be undone
