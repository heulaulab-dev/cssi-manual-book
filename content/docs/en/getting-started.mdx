# Getting Started

Follow these steps to understand the CSSI application ecosystem and find the right starting point for your work.

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Step 1 - Sign in

All users begin by signing in to CSSI using their organizational credentials.

After successful authentication, all users are directed to **Portal**.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 2 - Start from Portal

Portal is the central entry point for CSSI. From here you can:

View a consolidated dashboard with information from across CSSI applications. Access internal announcements from Intranet. See summaries of development requests, operational checklists, and other activity. Navigate directly into any CSSI application.

See the [Portal documentation](/docs/portal) for details on the dashboard and navigation.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 3 - Find the application you need

CSSI is composed of six applications, each with a distinct focus:

**Portal:** Central dashboard and cross-application entry point.

**CRM:** Customer, account, sales, and trading activities.

**Flowra:** Account opening and financial onboarding workflows.

**Daily Operation:** Operational checklists, approvals, and reporting.

**ITQM:** IT quality management and development request workflows.

**Intranet:** Internal information, regulations, and company communication.

Select the application most relevant to your current task and review its documentation.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 4 - Continue in the relevant application

Detailed workflows belong to each application's own documentation. Portal provides the overview; the individual applications handle the step-by-step processes.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Available applications and dashboard information may differ depending on your
  organizational access.
</Callout>
