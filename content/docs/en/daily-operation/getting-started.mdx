## What the sidebar contains

The sidebar organizes features by division. The **IT Division** section includes:

| Navigation Item | Description |
|---|---|
| **Today** | Complete and submit daily checklists |
| **Approvals** | Review and approve or reject submissions (supervisors) |
| **History** | Browse approved checklist records |
| **IT Config** | Configure tasks, parts, and checklist items (authorized users only) |

<Callout type="info">
  The navigation items available to you depend on your role and permissions.
  Contact your administrator if you cannot access a feature.
</Callout>
