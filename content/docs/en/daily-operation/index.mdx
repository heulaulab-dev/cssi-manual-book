# Daily Operation

Daily Operation supports operational checklists, approvals, and daily reporting within the CSSI ecosystem. It helps teams track and complete their routine operational tasks.

## What this application is for

Daily Operation provides the tools for creating, managing, and approving operational checklists, as well as generating daily reports.

## What you can do

- View operational checklists assigned to your division or role.
- Submit and approve checklists through the workflow.
- Track daily reporting and operational activity.
