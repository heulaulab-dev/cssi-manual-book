Daily Operation is the operational checklist workspace within the CSSI ecosystem. It supports daily task tracking, checklist submission, approval workflows, and historical reporting for operational teams.

## What this application is for

Daily Operation provides a structured environment for teams to manage routine operational tasks through a checklist-based workflow. It supports the full lifecycle: completing checklists, submitting them for review, approving or rejecting submissions, and reviewing historical records.

## What you can do

- **Complete daily checklists** — Mark checklist items as done, add comments, and record actions taken
- **Submit for approval** — Send completed parts of a checklist to your supervisor for review
- **Approve or reject submissions** — Review and act on pending checklist submissions from your team
- **Browse history** — View approved checklist records for any business date
- **Configure checklists** — Create and manage tasks, parts, and checklist items (authorized users only)

## Key concepts

- **Task** — A top-level grouping of related operational work (e.g., "End of Day Processing")
- **Part** — A sub-group within a task, representing a team or section responsible for specific checklist items
- **Checklist Item** — An individual item within a part that must be marked complete
- **Business Date** — The operational date for which checklists are being tracked

## Divisions

Daily Operation is organized by division. The **IT Division** is currently active, with additional divisions (Settlement, Risk Management) planned for future release.
