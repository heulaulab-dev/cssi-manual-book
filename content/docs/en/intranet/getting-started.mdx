### Step 1 — Open the application

Access Intranet from the Portal dashboard or through the top application navigation bar. Click the **Intranet** card on the Portal Dashboard, or select **Intranet** from the application switcher.

### Step 2 — Understand the workspace

When Intranet opens, you'll see the Dashboard with summary widgets showing announcements, regulations, notifications, and activity. The sidebar on the left provides navigation to all Intranet sections.

The sidebar is organized into three groups:

- **Dashboard** — Home (your main overview)
- **Intranet** — Announcements (create and manage announcements)
- **Regulation** — Compliance, HRD, Legal (browse and manage regulatory documents)

### Step 3 — Explore the Dashboard

Your Dashboard shows summary widgets for the most important information:

- **Intranet Announcements** — Recent company announcements with New and Urgent badges
- **Notifications** — Your latest alerts and messages
- **Regulations** — Summary of regulations across Compliance, HRD, and Legal categories
- **Development** — IT development request statuses
- **Daily Checklists** — Today's checklist submissions and their approval status

Each widget has a **View all** link at the bottom to open the full list in the relevant section.
