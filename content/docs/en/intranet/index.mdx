# Intranet

Intranet provides internal company information, regulations, and organizational communication within the CSSI ecosystem. It serves as the central hub for company-wide announcements and policy documents.

## What this application is for

Intranet delivers internal communications, regulations, and company resources to employees across the organization.

## What you can do

- Read internal announcements and company updates.
- Access regulations, policies, and regulatory documents.
- Browse company resources and organizational information.
