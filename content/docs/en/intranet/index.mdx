# Intranet

Intranet is the central hub for internal company information, regulations, and organizational communication within the CSSI ecosystem. It provides a unified space for company-wide announcements, policy documents, and compliance resources.

## What Intranet is for

- **Internal communications** — Read company announcements and updates from management
- **Regulations and policies** — Access Compliance, HRD, and Legal regulatory documents
- **Company resources** — Browse organizational information and division-specific documents
- **Administrative tools** — Manage users, roles, and system settings (for authorized users)

## What you can do

- View the Dashboard with summary widgets for announcements, regulations, and notifications
- Read and manage intranet announcements with publication scheduling
- Browse regulations organized by category: Compliance, HRD, and Legal
- Access regulation documents with version history, attachments, and effective periods
- Manage users, roles, audit logs, and system announcements (admin users)
- Manage regulation divisions and organizational structures
- Receive and manage notifications
- Manage your account settings and preferences

## How Intranet fits into CSSI

Intranet is the dedicated application for internal company information and compliance. While the Portal shows a summary of intranet announcements on its Dashboard, Intranet provides the full workspace for reading, managing, and organizing all internal communications and regulatory documents.

```
Intranet
  → central hub for internal information
  → manages announcements and regulations
  → provides administrative tools
```

## Getting around

Intranet is organized into four main areas:

- **Dashboard** — Your overview with widgets showing announcements, regulations, notifications, and activity summaries. This is where you'll spend most of your time.
- **Announcements** — Create, edit, and manage intranet announcements with publication scheduling and urgency flags.
- **Regulations** — Browse and manage regulatory documents organized by category: Compliance, HRD, and Legal.
- **Administration** — Manage users, roles, permissions, audit logs, system announcements, and divisions (available to authorized personnel only).
