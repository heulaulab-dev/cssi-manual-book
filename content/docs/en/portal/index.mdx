# Portal

Portal is the central entry point for the CSSI application ecosystem. After signing in, you arrive at the Dashboard, which provides a consolidated view of important information from across all CSSI applications.

## What Portal is for

- **Your starting point** — All users land here after login
- **Consolidated view** — Portal shows summaries from across CSSI applications without requiring you to open each application
- **Quick navigation** — Access all CSSI applications from one place
- **Important notices** — Key announcements are displayed prominently

## What you can do

- View your personalized Dashboard with widgets relevant to your role and division
- Read Intranet Announcements and System Announcements without leaving Portal
- Track development requests and daily checklist activity at a glance
- Monitor stock prices and trading rankings
- Quickly open any CSSI application
- Manage your account settings and preferences
- Receive and manage notifications
- Access administrative tools (if you have the appropriate permissions)

## How Portal fits into CSSI

Portal shows summaries so you can identify what needs attention. From there, you continue in the relevant application for detailed workflows.

```
Portal
  → shows summaries
  → you identify what needs attention
  → you continue in the relevant application
```

Portal is the starting point. Detailed workflows remain owned by their respective applications:

- Development belongs to **ITQM**
- Daily Checklists belong to **Daily Operation**
- Intranet Announcements belong to **Intranet**
- Stock and Rankings may belong to another application

## Getting around

The Portal is organized into three main areas:

- **Dashboard** — Your personalized overview with widgets and application shortcuts. This is where you'll spend most of your time.
- **Account** — Manage your profile, security settings, active sessions, and preferences like theme and notification settings.
- **Notifications** — View and filter announcements, security alerts, and system notifications.
- **Administration** — Manage users, roles, permissions, and system announcements (available to authorized personnel only).
