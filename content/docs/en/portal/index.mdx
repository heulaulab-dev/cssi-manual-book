# Portal

Portal is the central entry point for the CSSI application ecosystem. After signing in, users arrive here to view relevant information from across CSSI and access the applications they need.

## What Portal is for

- All users land here after login
- Portal provides a consolidated view across CSSI applications
- Dashboard content may differ depending on the user's division
- Portal acts as a launch point into other applications

## What you can do

- View your dashboard to see a consolidated view of information from across CSSI applications.
- See cross-application information without leaving Portal.
- Open CSSI applications such as CRM, Intranet, Flowra, ITQM, and Daily Operation.
- Continue work in the related application where the detailed workflow happens.

## How Portal fits into CSSI

Portal shows summaries so you can identify what needs attention. From there, you continue in the relevant application for detailed workflows.

```
Portal
  → shows summaries
  → you identify what needs attention
  → you continue in the relevant application
```

Portal is the starting point. Detailed workflows remain owned by their respective applications:

- Development belongs to **ITQM**
- Daily Checklists belong to **Daily Operation**
- Intranet Announcements belong to **Intranet**
- Stock and Rankings may belong to another application
