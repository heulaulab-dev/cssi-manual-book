## Intranet Announcements

The Intranet Announcements widget highlights internal and managerial information so you can see important updates without leaving Portal.

- This is a summary view
- Detailed announcement content belongs to Intranet

## Development

The Development widget gives you a quick view of development requests and their current status.

Visible status categories:

- In Progress
- Pending Approval
- Pending Assignment
- Pending IT Accept
- Pending Verification

## Daily Checklists

The Daily Checklists widget gives users a quick view of operational checklist activity relevant to their division.

Visible status categories:

- All
- Pending
- Approved
- Rejected
