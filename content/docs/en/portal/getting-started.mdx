# Getting Started

Follow these steps to get oriented in Portal.

## Sign in and enter Portal

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Step 1 - Sign in

Enter your credentials and sign in to CSSI. All users are directed to Portal after successful login.

> **Screenshot placeholder**
>
> Add screenshot: Login success leading to the Portal dashboard.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 2 - Review your dashboard

The Portal dashboard displays widgets with summaries from various CSSI applications. The widgets shown may differ depending on your division and available information.

> **Screenshot placeholder**
>
> Add screenshot: Portal dashboard showing visible widgets for one user/division.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 3 - Open information that needs attention

Click on a widget to see more detail or navigate to the related application. Widgets provide summaries from related CSSI applications.

> **Screenshot placeholder**
>
> Add screenshot: Portal widget with a visible list or actionable status.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 4 - Continue in the related application

Portal is the starting point. Once you identify what needs attention, open the related application to complete the workflow. Detailed workflows happen in the application, not in Portal.

>
> Add screenshot: Widget action or application shortcut leading into another CSSI application.

  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout type="info">
  Your dashboard may look different from another user's dashboard because some
  widgets are available only to specific divisions.
</Callout>
