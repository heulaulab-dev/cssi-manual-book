## First-time setup

If this is your first time signing in, you may be prompted to change your password. This is a security measure to ensure your account is protected.

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Step 1 — Change your password

When you first sign in, you will be redirected to a password reset page. Enter your temporary password, choose a new secure password (at least 8 characters), and confirm it.

> **Screenshot placeholder**
>
> Add screenshot: First-time password reset form.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 2 — Continue to your dashboard

After successfully changing your password, you will be redirected to the Portal Dashboard.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to do next

- Explore your **Dashboard** widgets to understand what information is available to you
- Check the **Applications** section at the bottom of the dashboard to access other CSSI apps
- Click on your name in the top-right corner to access **Account Settings**
- Click the **bell icon** to view your notifications

<Callout type="info">
  Your dashboard may look different from another user's dashboard because some
  widgets are available only to specific divisions and roles.
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
  If you cannot access certain applications or features, please contact your
  administrator to request the appropriate permissions.
</Callout>
