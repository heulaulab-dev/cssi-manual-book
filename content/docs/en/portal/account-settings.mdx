# Account Settings

Your Account settings page lets you manage your profile information, security credentials, active sessions, and application preferences. Access it by clicking your **profile icon or name** in the top-right corner of the page, then selecting **Account**.

## Profile

The Profile tab lets you manage your personal information.

### Editing your profile

1. Click the **Edit Profile** button
2. Update your **First Name**, **Last Name**, or **Primary email** as needed
3. Click **Save Changes** to apply your updates, or **Cancel** to discard them

### Your username

Your username is displayed for reference but cannot be changed through this interface. It is managed by your administrator.

## Security

The Security tab lets you change your password.

### Changing your password

1. Enter your **Current Password**
2. Enter a **New Password** (at least 8 characters)
3. Re-enter the new password in **Confirm New Password** to verify it matches
4. Click **Change Password**

## Sessions

The Sessions tab shows where you are logged in and lets you manage active sessions.

### Current session

Your current session is always shown at the top. It displays:

- **Device** — The device you are using
- **Browser** — Your web browser
- **IP address** — Your network address
- **Active** — How long your current session has been open

### Other sessions

If you are logged in from other devices, they will appear under **Other Sessions**. For each session, you can see:

- **Device** — The device name
- **IP address** — The network address

### Managing sessions

- Click **Revoke** next to any other session to sign out that device
- Click **Sign out all others** to revoke all sessions except your current one

This is useful if you suspect your account may have been accessed from an unfamiliar device.

## Preferences

The Preferences tab lets you customize your Portal experience.

### Theme mode

Choose how the Dashboard looks:

| Theme | Description |
|---|---|
| **Dark** | Dark background with light text |
| **Light** | Light background with dark text |
| **Classic Dark** | A darker, high-contrast theme |
| **System** | Automatically matches your computer's theme setting |

### Sidebar behavior

Choose how the left sidebar behaves:

| Behavior | Description |
|---|---|
| **Expanded** | Sidebar is always open |
| **Collapsed** | Sidebar is minimized; click to expand |
| **Expand on hover** | Sidebar expands when you move your mouse near the edge |

### Notification preferences

Control which types of notifications you receive:

| Setting | Description |
|---|---|
| **Email Notifications** | Receive notifications via email |
| **Push Notifications** | Receive browser push notifications |
| **SMS Notifications** | Receive notifications via text message |

Toggle each setting on or off using the switch next to it.
