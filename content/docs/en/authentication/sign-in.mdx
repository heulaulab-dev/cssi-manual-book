  <Step>

### Step 2 - Enter your Employee ID

Enter the Employee ID assigned to your account.

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 3 - Enter your password

Returning users enter their current password. First-time users enter the default password provided for their account.

<Callout type="info">
  If this is your first sign-in, CSSI will require you to create a new password
  before you can continue. See [First-Time Password
  Change](./first-time-password).
</Callout>

  </Step>
  <Step>

### Step 4 - Sign in

After successful authentication:

Returning users are directed to Portal. First-time users are directed to the password-change flow.

  </Step>
</Steps>

## After you sign in

**Returning user:**

```
Sign In → Portal
```

**First-time user:**

```
Sign In → Change Password → Sign In Again → Portal
```

See [Portal documentation](/docs/portal) for what to do once you arrive.
