# Stock

The Stock page provides a split-view interface for looking up stock symbols and viewing detailed price information. It is accessible from the sidebar under the **Sales** group.

## How the Stock page works

The Stock page is divided into two sections:

- **Stock list** (left side) — A searchable table of stock symbols and security names
- **Stock detail** (right side) — Price information for the selected stock

When you first open the page, the detail panel shows a placeholder message: **"Select a stock — Choose a stock from the list to view its price information."**

## Finding a stock

1. Use the **search box** at the top of the stock list to filter by stock code or name
2. Click on any stock row to select it
3. The detail panel updates to show price information for the selected stock

## Stock detail information

When you select a stock, the detail panel displays:

- **Stock code and name** — The selected stock's identifier and full name
- **Current price** — The latest closing price in Indonesian Rupiah
- **Price change** — The absolute and percentage change, color-coded (green for positive, red for negative)
- **Price chart** — An interactive chart showing price history over time
- **Time range selector** — Choose different periods to view (1D, 5D, 1M, 3M, 1Y)

## Clearing your selection

Click the **clear button** in the detail panel to deselect the current stock and return to the placeholder state. You can then select a different stock from the list.
