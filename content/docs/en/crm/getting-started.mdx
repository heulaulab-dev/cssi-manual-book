# Getting Started

Follow these steps to get oriented in CRM.

## Open CRM

<Steps>
  <Step>

### Step 1 — Open the application

Access CRM from the Portal dashboard or through the top application navigation bar. Click the **app switcher** in the top-left corner and select **CRM**.

### Step 2 — Review the Dashboard

When CRM opens, you land on the Dashboard. The Dashboard shows an overview of sales activity, stock data, and performance rankings through a series of widgets.

### Step 3 — Explore the sidebar

The sidebar on the left organizes CRM into three groups:

- **Dashboard** — Return to your overview at any time
- **Sales** — Access accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, prospects, and stock
- **Trend Catch** — Review P/L rankings and trading rankings

Click any item in the sidebar to navigate to that section.
