# CRM

CRM is the workspace for customer, account, sales, and trading-related activities within the CSSI ecosystem. It provides tools for managing client relationships and tracking sales operations.

## What this application is for

CRM supports day-to-day customer and account management, including sales tracking and trading-related workflows.

## What you can do

- Manage customer accounts by viewing and updating customer information and account details.
- Track sales activity and monitor opportunities.
- Support trading operations with related customer and trading information.
