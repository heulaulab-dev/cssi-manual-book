# CRM

CRM is the workspace for sales, trading, and customer relationship management within the CSSI ecosystem. It provides tools for managing client relationships, tracking sales activity, monitoring stock data, and reviewing trading performance.

## What CRM is for

- **Sales management** — Track leads, prospects, opportunities, accounts, and contacts through the sales pipeline
- **Stock reference** — Look up stock symbols, view price charts, and monitor market data
- **Performance rankings** — Review profit/loss and trading activity rankings
- **Customer relationships** — Maintain detailed account and contact records with associated activities and documents

## What you can do

- Manage customer accounts by viewing and updating account details, balances, and associated records
- Track sales activity through leads, prospects, opportunities, and contacts
- Look up stock symbols and view price history with interactive charts
- Review P/L rankings and trading rankings filtered by date, segment, and type
- Access your account settings, notifications, and administrative tools

## How CRM fits into CSSI

CRM is where detailed sales and trading workflows happen. Portal shows summaries so you can identify what needs attention, then you continue in CRM for the full workflow.

```
Portal (summaries) → CRM (detailed workflows)
```

## Getting around

CRM is organized into three main areas accessible from the sidebar:

- **Dashboard** — Your overview with sales metrics, stock data, and rankings
- **Sales** — Manage accounts, contacts, leads, opportunities, prospects, and stock
- **Trend Catch** — Review P/L rankings and trading rankings
