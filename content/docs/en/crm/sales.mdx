# Sales

The Sales section of CRM is where you manage customer relationships and track sales activity. It contains six pages accessible from the sidebar under the **Sales** group.

## Accounts

The Accounts page lists trading accounts with their codes, services, statuses, balances, and ownership. Use this page to manage your client accounts.

**What you can do:**

- View a list of all accounts with search and filter capabilities
- Click **Create account** to add a new account
- Click on any account row to view its full details
- Edit account information from the detail view

**Account detail sections:**

- Overview — Account summary and key information
- Identity & Contact — Personal details and contact information
- Addresses — Physical and mailing addresses
- Balances — Account balance information
- Communication Preferences — How the account holder prefers to be contacted
- Bank Details — Banking information
- Documents — Attached files and documents
- Activities — Activity history and notes
- Cases — Support cases associated with the account
- Leads — Leads linked to this account
- Opportunities — Opportunities linked to this account
- Organizations — Organizational affiliations
- E-Campaign — Email campaign participation

The Contacts page lists people linked to accounts. Use this page to manage contact information including phone numbers, email addresses, ownership details, and history.

**What you can do:**

- View a list of all contacts with search and filter capabilities
- Click **Create contact** to add a new contact
- Click on any contact row to view their full profile
- Edit contact information from the detail view

**Contact detail sections:**

- Overview — Contact summary and key information
- Direct Reports — People reporting to this contact
- Documents — Attached files and documents
- Cases — Support cases associated with the contact
- Bugs — Issues reported by or related to this contact
- Other — Additional information
- Activities — Activity history and notes

## Common actions across Sales pages

All Sales pages share common patterns:

- **Search** — Type in the search box to filter results by text
- **Filters** — Use dropdown selectors to narrow results by status, type, or other criteria
- **Create** — Click the create button in the page header to add a new record
- **View** — Click any row to open the full detail view
- **Edit** — Edit records from the detail view (requires appropriate permissions)

<Callout type="info">
  The Sales pages and actions available to you depend on your role and
  permissions. Create and edit buttons only appear if you have the required
  access. Contact your administrator if you need additional permissions.
</Callout>
