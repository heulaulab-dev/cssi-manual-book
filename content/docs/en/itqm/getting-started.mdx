### Step 2 — Understand the workspace

When ITQM opens, you'll see the **Dashboard** as your starting page. The dashboard displays KPI cards, workload information, and upcoming deadlines relevant to your role.

The left sidebar provides navigation to all ITQM sections:

- **Dashboard** — Your overview with metrics and activity
- **Develop Request** — Submit and track development requests
- **Develop Accept** — Review and assign incoming requests (IT approver role)
- **Develop Approve** — Approve or reject requests (division approver role)
- **Done Report** — View completed work and verify completion
- **Issues** — Track issues in list or Kanban view
- **Config** — Manage request and system types (authorized users only)

### Step 3 — Start with the Dashboard

Review your **Dashboard** to understand your current workload:

- Check the **KPI cards** at the top for a summary of request counts by status
- Look at the **Development Lifecycle** widget to see the overall pipeline
- Review **Upcoming Due Dates** to see what needs attention soon
- Check the **Team Workload** widget to see who is working on what

From the dashboard, you can navigate to any section using the sidebar.
