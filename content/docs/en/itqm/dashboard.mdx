# Dashboard

The ITQM Dashboard gives you a personalized overview of your development workload, pending actions, and team activity. It is the first page you see when opening ITQM.

## Greeting

When you load the Dashboard, you'll see a personalized greeting based on the current time of day. A **Refresh** button is available to reload the widget data.

## KPI Cards

At the top of the Dashboard, a row of KPI cards provides a quick summary of development requests by status. Each card shows the count and label for a specific status.

| Card | Description |
|---|---|
| Draft | Requests still being prepared, not yet submitted |
| Pending Approval | Requests awaiting approval from the requestor or business unit |
| Revision | Requests being revised based on feedback |
| Pending IT Accept | Requests awaiting IT acceptance after completion |
| Pending Assignment | Waiting for an IT developer to be assigned |
| In Progress | Work actively being performed |
| Pending Verification | Waiting for verification by the requestor |
| Done | Completed and closed requests |

## Development Lifecycle

The Development Lifecycle widget visualizes the pipeline of requests across all workflow stages. It shows a horizontal flow from Draft through to Done, with the count of requests at each stage.

This gives you a quick understanding of where requests are concentrated and whether any stages have bottlenecks.

## Upcoming Due Dates

The Upcoming Due Dates widget lists development requests that are approaching their due dates. Each entry shows the request subject, the assigned person, and the due date.

- Items are sorted by due date, with the nearest deadlines first
- Overdue items are highlighted to draw attention
- Click on a request to view its full details

## Team Workload

The Team Workload widget shows a breakdown of work assigned to each team member. It helps you understand current capacity and identify who has availability for new work.

- Each row shows the team member's name and their count of active requests by status
- The workload is visualized with status-colored indicators

## Intranet Announcements

The Intranet Announcements widget displays internal announcements relevant to your division. It shows the most recent announcements with title, excerpt, and date.

- Click on an announcement to read the full content
- Use the **View all** link to open the complete announcement list in Intranet

- Each entry shows the request subject, system, requester, and completion date

## Daily Checklists

The Daily Checklists widget provides a summary of daily operational checklist activity. Each entry shows the checklist name, status, and submission details.

- Use the category tabs to filter by status: **All**, **Pending**, **Approved**, or **Rejected**
- Click on a checklist entry to view details in Daily Operation

## Status Breakdown Chart

The Status Breakdown chart provides a visual distribution of your requests across all workflow statuses. It uses a pie or donut chart format to show the proportion of requests in each status.

This helps you quickly assess whether most of your requests are in progress, pending, or completed.

## Navigating from the Dashboard

From any widget, you can click on an entry to navigate directly to the relevant detail page. You can also use the left sidebar to move between ITQM sections:

- **Develop Request** — Submit and manage requests
- **Develop Accept** — Review and assign requests (IT approver role)
- **Develop Approve** — Approve or reject requests (division approver role)
- **Done Report** — View and verify completed work
- **Issues** — Track issues in list or Kanban view

  The widgets and data shown on your dashboard may vary depending on your role
  and division. If you need access to additional information, contact your
